    Methods, units & screening limits

    Texture. USDA primary classes use sand, silt and clay percentages on the fine-earth basis. Totals within 0.5 percentage points of 100 are normalized and flagged. Gravel and USCS fines are separate measurements.

    Gradation. Passing uses recovered dry mass including the pan. The signed closure is 100 × (recovered / initial − 1). D-values use linear interpolation in percent passing against log aperture, with no extrapolation. Cu = D60/D10 and Cc = D30²/(D10×D60). mm is the output unit.

    USCS. Uses measured No. 4 and No. 200 where required, an inorganic specimen, and the <75 mm basis. PI = LL − PL and A-line = 0.73 × (LL − 20). Engineering design requires site data, applicable standards and qualified review. Organic groups, peat and detailed group-name modifiers are outside scope.

    USDA NRCS Soil Survey Manual, Chapter 3 · FHWA USCS guidance, Table 4-13 · ASTM D2487
